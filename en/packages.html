Ask about Package C
  • The final list of examinations follows the guide sheet issued on the day.
  • Items marked for men or women apply according to sex. The gynaecology items in Package A are for married women.
  • Item counts follow the package lists. Items for men and for women are counted separately, and the lists for Packages B and C also include the blood draw, the physician's overall review and breakfast, so each guest completes fewer items than the headline number.
  • In Package A, the digital X-ray, colour Doppler ultrasound and digital liver ultrasound come without printed images or film.
  • 待确认：体检报告的语言、出具时间、交付方式，以及是否提供报告解读
  • 待确认：C 套餐清单未列 B 套餐的前列腺癌三项、胰功能三项；B 套餐清单未列 A 套餐的心功能三项。请向体检中心确认是否确实不含
  • 待确认：清单中的营养早餐（B、C 套餐）和健康餐（A 套餐）是否就是中午的体检中心自助餐
  • 待确认：妇科“已婚项目”的具体适用规则；B、C 套餐的妇科、TCT、HPV 是否同样适用
