The day

How the day runs, from morning to evening

The medical check-up and the TCM experience are scheduled on the same day. Lunch is served inside the check-up centre, so you do not need to go elsewhere to eat, and you only book once.

  1. Morning

    Fasting medical check-up

    Arrive without having eaten. You complete the blood draw, imaging and the other examinations in your package. The final list follows the guide sheet you receive on the day.

    Packages A / B / C

  2. Noon

    Buffet lunch at the centre

    After the examinations, lunch is a buffet inside the check-up centre. There is no transfer, and the afternoon follows on directly.

    Lunch included

  3. Afternoon

    TCM experience

    A TCM consultation with pulse and tongue diagnosis, then any three of five therapies: acupuncture, cupping, foot steaming, tuina massage and moxibustion.

    Choose 3 of 5

  4. Evening

    Dinner at a Cantonese or halal restaurant

    Dinner is arranged at a Cantonese tea restaurant or a halal restaurant. The menu is confirmed with you in advance, according to your dietary habits and restrictions.

    Dinner included

Everything is completed in one day, with no second appointment. Lunch and dinner are both included in the package.

The check-up is in the morning on an empty stomach, so we suggest arriving in Shenzhen the day before. The day ends after dinner, so we suggest leaving the next day or later. Accommodation before and after is not part of the package price.

Choose how thorough the check-up is. The TCM session and both meals are included.

All three packages include the morning medical check-up, the afternoon TCM experience, lunch and dinner. They differ only in the depth of the morning check-up. Prices depend on how many people travel together.

Can the check-up and the TCM experience really be done in one day?

Yes. You have the fasting check-up in the morning, a buffet lunch at the check-up centre, the TCM consultation and therapies in the afternoon, and dinner in the evening. It is all on the same day, with no second appointment.

What is the difference between the three packages?

All three include the morning medical check-up, the afternoon TCM experience and both meals. They differ in the depth of the morning check-up: Package A is a basic screening of 23 items; Package B has 52 items in 4 categories plus 3 added tests; Package C has 62 items in 4 categories plus 5 added tests. The packages page lists every item.

What does the price include?

The morning medical check-up for your chosen package, the buffet lunch at the check-up centre, the afternoon TCM experience (consultation plus three of five therapies), dinner, and an English-speaking escort all day. Other costs beyond these, such as flights, visa and hotel, are not part of the package price. 待确认：是否可代订机票、酒店或安排接送；各场地之间的交通是否包含

How is the price worked out for a group?

Prices are set by the number of people travelling together (1 to 4), and the price per person falls as the group grows. For example, Package A costs IDR 14.4 million for one guest, and IDR 48.6 million in total for four guests, which is IDR 12.2 million per person. 待确认：5 人及以上如何计价

What happens in the TCM experience?

A registered TCM physician first sees you one to one for a consultation with pulse and tongue diagnosis. You then choose three of five therapies: acupuncture, cupping, foot steaming, tuina massage and moxibustion. It takes place in a private treatment room at a licensed TCM clinic, and acupuncture needles are single-use.

Confirm your package, group size and date with us on WhatsApp. A IDR 1.8 million scheduling fee is collected when the date is confirmed. Do I need a visa to visit Shenzhen?

Indonesian passport holders normally need a visa, arranged in advance, to enter mainland China. Since 12 June 2025 Indonesian nationals have also been eligible for China’s 240-hour visa-free transit policy: you need a valid travel document and an onward ticket with a confirmed date and seat to a third country or region, and you must enter through a designated port and stay within the permitted area. Rules can change, and whether the policy applies depends on your exact route, so please confirm with the Chinese embassy or consulate in Indonesia, the visa application centre or your airline before you book.
