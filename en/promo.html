Can the check-up and the TCM experience really be done in one day?

Yes. You have the fasting check-up in the morning, a buffet lunch at the check-up centre, the TCM consultation and therapies in the afternoon, and dinner in the evening. It is all on the same day, with no second appointment.

What does the price include?

The morning medical check-up for your chosen package, the buffet lunch at the check-up centre, the afternoon TCM experience (consultation plus three of five therapies), dinner, an English-speaking escort all day, and airport pickup and drop-off. Other costs beyond these, such as flights, visa and hotel, are not part of the package price. If you wish, our one-stop service arranges flights, hotel and visa for you (price on request). 待确认：接送机适用哪些机场或口岸；各场地之间的交通是否包含

Can halal meals be arranged?

Dinner can be arranged at a halal restaurant. The menu follows halal requirements and is confirmed with you in advance. Please also tell us about allergies and dietary restrictions beforehand. 待确认：体检中心自助午餐是否有清真或素食选择

Do I need a visa to visit Shenzhen?

Indonesian passport holders normally need a visa, arranged in advance, to enter mainland China. Since 12 June 2025 Indonesian nationals have also been eligible for China’s 240-hour visa-free transit policy: you need a valid travel document and an onward ticket with a confirmed date and seat to a third country or region, and you must enter through a designated port and stay within the permitted area. Rules can change, and whether the policy applies depends on your exact route, so please confirm with the Chinese embassy or consulate in Indonesia, the visa application centre or your airline before you book. (Checked in November 2026.) If you choose our one-stop service, we help with the visa application and book your flights and hotel (price on request).

How do I book, and can I cancel or reschedule?

Confirm your package, group size and date with us on WhatsApp. A IDR 1.8 million scheduling fee is collected when the date is confirmed. More than 7 days before the appointment you can cancel free of charge or reschedule once free of charge. From 3 to 7 days before, cancelling or rescheduling costs IDR 900,000. Less than 3 days before, the scheduling fee is not refunded.
