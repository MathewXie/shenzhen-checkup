The day

How the day runs, from morning to evening

The medical check-up and the TCM experience are scheduled on the same day. Lunch is served inside the check-up centre, so you do not need to go elsewhere to eat, and you only book once.

  1. Morning

    Fasting medical check-up

    Please fast on the morning of your check-up. You complete the blood draw, imaging and the other examinations in your package. The final list follows the guide sheet you receive on the day.

    Packages A / B / C

  2. Noon

    Buffet lunch at the centre

    After the examinations, lunch is a buffet inside the check-up centre. There is no transfer, and the afternoon follows on directly.

    Lunch included

  3. Afternoon

    TCM experience

    A TCM consultation with pulse and tongue diagnosis, then any three of five therapies: acupuncture, dry cupping (no bloodletting), foot steaming, tuina massage and moxibustion.

    Choose 3 of 5

  4. Evening

    Dinner at a Cantonese or halal restaurant

    Dinner is arranged at a Cantonese tea restaurant or a halal restaurant. The menu is confirmed with you in advance, according to your dietary habits and restrictions.

    Dinner included

Everything is completed in one day, with no second appointment. Lunch and dinner are both included in the package.

The check-up is in the morning on an empty stomach, so we suggest arriving in Shenzhen the day before. The day ends after dinner, so we suggest leaving the next day or later. Please book your own flights and accommodation; they are not part of the package.

Choose how thorough the check-up is. The TCM session and both meals are included.

All three packages include the morning medical check-up, the afternoon TCM experience, lunch and dinner. They differ only in the depth of the morning check-up. Prices depend on how many people travel together.

FAQ

What you may want to know before you travel

Can the check-up and the TCM experience really be done in one day?

Yes. You have the fasting check-up in the morning, a buffet lunch at the check-up centre, the TCM consultation and therapies in the afternoon, and dinner in the evening. It is all on the same day, with no second appointment.

What is the difference between the three packages?

All three include the morning medical check-up, the afternoon TCM experience and both meals. They differ in the depth of the morning check-up: Package A is a basic screening of 23 items; Package B has 52 items in 4 categories plus 3 added tests; Package C has 62 items in 4 categories plus 5 added tests. The packages page lists every item.

What does the price include?

The morning medical check-up for your chosen package, the buffet lunch at the check-up centre, the afternoon TCM experience (consultation plus three of five therapies), dinner, and English-speaking staff to accompany you all day. Flights, hotels and visas are not included and we do not book them; please arrange them yourself. 待确认：各场地之间的交通是否包含

How is the price worked out for a group?

Prices are set by the number of people travelling together (1 to 4), and the price per person falls as the group grows. For example, Package A costs US$800 for one guest, and US$2,700 in total for four guests, which is US$675 per person. For groups of five or more, please contact our customer service.

How should I prepare for the check-up?

Please fast on the morning of your check-up. The final list of examinations follows the guide sheet issued on the day. 待确认：行前须知（空腹时长、用药、女性生理期、孕期、体内金属植入物等）

What happens in the TCM experience?

A registered TCM physician first sees you one to one for a consultation with pulse and tongue diagnosis. You then choose three of five therapies: acupuncture, dry cupping (no bloodletting), foot steaming, tuina massage and moxibustion. It takes place in a private treatment room at a licensed TCM clinic, and acupuncture needles are single-use.

Can halal meals be arranged?

Dinner can be arranged at a halal restaurant. The menu follows halal requirements and is confirmed with you in advance. Please also tell us about allergies and dietary restrictions beforehand. 待确认：体检中心自助午餐是否有清真或素食选择

What language do the staff who accompany me speak?

They speak English and accompany you throughout the day: check-in, the examinations, ordering meals and the TCM session. 待确认：除英语外，是否可提供印尼语或中文陪同、翻译

How do I book, and can I cancel or reschedule?

Confirm your package, group size and date with us on WhatsApp. A US$100 scheduling fee is collected when the date is confirmed. More than 7 days before the appointment you can cancel free of charge or reschedule once free of charge. From 3 to 7 days before, cancelling or rescheduling costs US$50. Less than 3 days before, the scheduling fee is not refunded.

Where does the check-up take place?

The medical check-up takes place at a partner check-up centre in Shenzhen, and the TCM experience at a licensed TCM clinic.
