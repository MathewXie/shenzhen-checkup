Who runs this website

What this website collects

You do not need an account to use this website.

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

When you send the enquiry form, your answers (name, WhatsApp number, package, group size, expected date, travel wishes and message) go to our enquiry form on Google Forms, a service provided by Google, and are kept in our Google account so that our team can reply to you on WhatsApp. We use them only to answer your enquiry and arrange your trip.

Information you give us on WhatsApp

What you send us on WhatsApp, such as your name, contact details, group size, preferred date, dietary habits and restrictions, is used only to answer your enquiry, confirm your schedule and arrange your day.

Personal and health information needed for the check-up and the TCM experience is collected from you by the check-up centre and the TCM clinic when they provide the service.

待确认：信息保存期限、是否向第三方提供、跨境传输的依据，以及您查询、更正、删除信息的途径

About medical content

This website describes a service arrangement. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a promise of any result.

The medical check-up is carried out by a partner check-up centre in Shenzhen, and the TCM experience is provided by registered physicians at a licensed TCM clinic. Results and medical judgement rest with the reports they issue and the physicians' opinions.

The TCM session is a wellness experience and does not replace the diagnosis or treatment of any condition. If you feel unwell or are receiving treatment, please consult your own doctor first.

Prices and booking

Prices on this website are in US dollars and are package prices for 1 to 4 people travelling together. The final list of examinations follows the guide sheet issued on the day.

A US$100 scheduling fee is collected when the date is confirmed. The cancellation and rescheduling rules are set out under Booking on the home page.
