Before your check-up

How to prepare for the morning check-up

These are the check-up centre's instructions. If any of them applies to you, tell us on WhatsApp before you book and we will check your package with the centre.

Arrival

  • Arrive at your booked time, between 8:00 and 10:00. Blood is not drawn after 10:30.
  • Bring your passport.

Food and drink

  • No food after 8 pm the evening before, and no water after 10 pm.
  • On the morning of the check-up, do not eat or drink anything, including water, until the fasting tests are done.
  • For three days before: eat light meals, avoid alcohol, rest well and avoid hard exercise, so that the results are accurate.

Toilet and full bladder

  • You can use the toilet when you get up.
  • Some ultrasound scans (bladder, uterus and ovaries, prostate) need a full bladder. You do not need to hold it from the morning: after the fasting tests, drink some warm water and wait until your bladder is full.

Medicines

  • Do not take medicines that can affect the liver or kidneys before the check-up.
  • If you take regular medicine for a long-term condition such as diabetes or high blood pressure, keep taking it as usual. If you are unsure about a medicine, ask your own doctor.

Pregnancy and women's health

  • If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or you (woman or man) plan a pregnancy within the next six months, you cannot have X-ray or CT scans.
  • Gynaecological examinations are only for women who have had sexual intercourse.
  • Women should avoid their period: book the check-up at least 3 days after it has ended.

What to wear

  • If your package includes eye-pressure or slit-lamp tests, do not wear contact lenses.
  • Wear trousers, and avoid clothes or jewellery with metal.
